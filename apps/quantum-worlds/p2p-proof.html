PeerJS comes from jsDelivr; the public PeerJS broker and STUN help set up the connection. No TURN relay is configured. Networks that require one cannot connect here. IP-based locations from ipapi.co are approximate and shared with your connected peer; they are not GPS fixes. A local file:// link only works where the same file path exists; for another device, open its copy with ?peer= and your peer ID, or share a hosted copy.
*One-way delay is the difference between two machines' timestamps. It includes clock offset, can be negative, and is neither a ping nor a bound on network latency.
