About

A self-contained, cycle-accurate NES emulator. Full 6502 core (official + illegal opcodes), loopy-scroll PPU with sprite-0 hit and overflow quirks, non-linearly mixed APU (2 pulse + triangle + noise + DMC), mappers 0/1/2/3/4/7/66 with MMC3 A12 IRQ, a real debugger, and a WebGL CRT shader.

All data stays in your browser (localStorage + IndexedDB for battery SRAM). Works offline. No ROMs are embedded — built-in content is generated at runtime.
