Field manual

Each sector is a seeded relief model. Pale, tightly packed contours mark steep slopes. The trace shows an east-west slice through your selected point.

Choose a station, or click the chart to place a sounding. Deploy the probe to record its depth. With audio on, deeper returns sound lower and arrive later.

New sector charts another seabed. Use ↗ in the log to revisit any recorded sounding from this session.
