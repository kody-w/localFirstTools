Watch one invented language turn into a whole family of languages

A small group of villages speaks one invented proto-language. Press play and they spread across an unexplored continent. Sound changes such as p → f between vowels start in one village and travel outward to its neighbours. Mountains slow them down and seas stop them. Words get borrowed, replaced, made into compounds and shift in meaning. Over thousands of years, dialects grow into separate languages and languages into families.

  • Click any village to see its lexicon and trace every word back to its proto-form.
  • Raise mountains or flood valleys to cut populations apart, then watch them drift away from each other.
  • Start conquests, migrations and plagues. Isogloss maps, sound-law waves and a live family tree show what happens.
  • Translate sentences into every living language and hear them pronounced.
