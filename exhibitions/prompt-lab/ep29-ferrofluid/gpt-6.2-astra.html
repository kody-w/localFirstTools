Experiment 001

Inside the field

A normal magnetic field concentrates at bumps in the liquid. Above a threshold, magnetic stress overcomes gravity and surface tension, raising a closely spaced array of peaks.

This visual model carries that pattern onto a suspended drop. Field gradients stretch its body; rounded necks narrow and reunite. Suspension and branching are speculative, not a calibrated fluid simulation.

References: Cowley & Rosensweig, The interfacial stability of a ferromagnetic fluid (1967); Adaptalux macro study, for diffused reflections on black liquid.
